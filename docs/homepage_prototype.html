A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A few things to know before bringing your conversations together.

What does WhatsMine bring together?

Customer messaging, contacts, AI assistants, campaigns, visual automations, social publishing and ecommerce context in a business workspace.

Can my team work together in the inbox?

The inbox includes conversation assignment, internal notes, labels and bot/human handover to help coordinate customer support.

How does the AI assistant learn about my business?

You can add knowledge sources, configure chatbot instructions and test replies in the playground. Provider configuration is required, and generated answers should be reviewed.

Can I connect my ecommerce store?

The project includes Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce connection paths. Available operations depend on store configuration and platform capabilities.
