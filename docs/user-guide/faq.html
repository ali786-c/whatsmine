FAQ & Troubleshooting

Fast fixes for the questions we hear most. Click a question to expand the answer.

Channels & connections

My WhatsApp QR session disconnected. What do I do?

Open Inbox → Channel Setup, find the disconnected session and re-scan the QR code with your phone (WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link a Device). Sessions drop when the phone stays offline too long or the linked device is removed.

Why can't my agent reply to this customer?

Most likely the 24-hour window closed — free-form replies are only allowed within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Send an approved template message to re-open the conversation (Instagram allows tagged replies for up to 7 days).

Instagram won't connect.

Your Instagram account must be a Business or Creator (professional) account — personal accounts can't connect. Convert in the Instagram app under Settings, then retry the connection from Channel Setup.

AI & chatbot

The chatbot answers “contact our team” even though the info is in my knowledge base.

Three usual causes:

  1. The document was edited but not re-indexed — re-save it in the KB.
  2. No embed-capable provider is configured — add one under AI → Providers, then re-index.
  3. The test question isn't literally covered by the KB text — bots answer from what's written, not what's implied. Add a clear Q&A for it.
How do I stop the bot from answering completely?

Deactivate the chatbot under AI → Chatbots. Once a customer has asked for a human (or an agent has replied), the bot stays out of that conversation.

My microphone doesn't work for voice notes.

The browser needs permission: click the lock icon in the address bar → Site settings → set Microphone to Allow, then reload the page. On Windows also check Settings → Privacy → Microphone if it still fails.

Campaigns & messaging

My campaign shows sent, but some contacts didn't receive it.

Check the recipient list on the campaign page. Common reasons: invalid or changed phone numbers, contacts who opted out, template rejections, or channel messaging limits. Fix the numbers, re-import or re-send to the affected segment.

Why was my template rejected?

Meta reviews templates for category accuracy and spam-like content. Common rejections: marketing content marked as “utility”, excessive caps/emojis, or missing opt-out language. Revise and resubmit with honest categorisation (see templates).

Account & billing

I hit a plan limit. What happens?

The app shows which limit you reached (channels, contacts, seats…) and offers an upgrade. Your data is safe — nothing is deleted; you just can't add beyond the limit until you upgrade or free up space.

How do I download invoices?

Go to Account → Billing, open the invoice and print/save it as PDF from your browser. See Team & Billing.

How do I delete my account or workspace data?

Workspace administrators can export data first (Settings → Data Export), then contact support via Support → Tickets to close the account. We take data removal seriously and confirm once it's done.

Still stuck?

🎫 Open a support ticket

From inside the app: Support → Tickets → New Ticket. Describe what you did, what you expected and what happened — screenshots help a lot, and tickets go straight to the team.
