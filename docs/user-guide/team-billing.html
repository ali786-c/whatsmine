Team & Billing

Bring your team on board with the right permissions, and manage your plan, payment methods and invoices.

Inviting teammates

1

Open Account → Team (workspace administrators only) and click Invite.

2

Enter the person's email and role, then send the invite.

3

They receive an email invitation — accepting it creates their login and adds them to the workspace.

Seats depend on your plan; the Team page shows how many are in use.

Roles & permissions

RoleCan do
AdministratorEverything — billing, team, channels, settings and all messaging features
Agent / MemberDay-to-day work — inbox conversations, contacts, campaigns per assigned permissions

Grant or restrict specific abilities per role (for example allow campaigns but hide billing) from the roles & permissions screen. Check the Audit Log to review who changed what.

Your subscription

Account → Subscription shows your current plan, renewal date and usage against plan limits — channels, contacts, messages and more. Upgrade or downgrade any time; changes apply from the next billing cycle or immediately, depending on the change.

📎 Plan limits

When you hit a limit (e.g. maximum connected channels), the app tells you which limit and offers an upgrade. Nothing breaks silently.

Choosing a plan

Open Account → Plans to compare what each tier includes: number of workspaces, channels, contacts, message volume and team seats. Pick the tier that matches your current size — you can grow later.

Billing & invoices

  • Account → Billing lists every charge with its date and amount.
  • Open an invoice to view or print it (PDF via your browser's print dialog) for accounting.
  • Update your payment method and billing details from the same page.
