Campaigns

Send offers, updates and announcements to hundreds or thousands of contacts — guided by a wizard that keeps you compliant.

The campaign wizard

Open Broadcasting → Campaigns and click New Campaign. The wizard walks you through five steps:

1

Channel — pick WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram. WhatsApp is the most common for broadcasts.

2

Audience — target contacts with filters: tags, segments, or all contacts. The wizard shows a live recipient count before you continue.

3

Content — write the message or pick an approved template and fill its variables. Add media (image, video, document) where the channel allows it.

4

Schedule — send immediately or pick a date and time. Scheduled campaigns run on their own; you can close the browser.

5

Review — check the summary (audience size, content, schedule) and launch.

💡 Warm up your sending

New WhatsApp numbers should start with smaller broadcasts and engaged audiences — quality and limits grow with good engagement. Create the “Campaign sent” automation before launching if you want per-recipient follow-ups.

Audience tips

  • Use segments to keep reusable audiences — e.g. “VIP customers”, “Karachi leads”.
  • Exclude recent non-deliveries by building segments on tags like undeliverable.
  • Always honour opt-outs — add contacts who reply STOP to an opt-out tag and exclude it in campaigns.

Tracking results

Open a campaign to see its live stats — sent, delivered, read and replied counts — plus the per-recipient list. Compare campaigns over time in Reports → Campaigns.

SMS & email senders

Campaigns can also go out over SMS and email once a sender is configured:

  • Broadcasting → SMS Gateways — connect an SMS provider with guided, step-by-step setup screens.
  • Broadcasting → Email Server — connect your SMTP with a built-in setup guide and test button.

Common questions

  • Can I send without a template? Only inside each customer's 24-hour reply window; outside it, WhatsApp requires an approved template.
  • Can I cancel a scheduled campaign? Yes — open it before the send time and cancel from the campaign page.
  • Why did some recipients not get it? Check the recipient list: invalid numbers, opt-outs and channel limits are the usual reasons.
