Contacts & Leads

Your audience database — import, organise, segment and find new leads to grow it.

The contacts list

Open Contacts to see everyone in this workspace: name, phone, email, tags and last activity. Use the search bar and tag filters to narrow the list, and click any contact to open their full profile.

The contact profile shows conversation history, tags, custom fields and notes — everything your team knows about this person in one screen.

Importing contacts

1

Click Import on the contacts page.

2

Upload a CSV or Excel file. Include at least a phone number; name and email are recommended.

3

Map your file's columns to contact fields, and optionally tag everyone in this import (e.g. imported-march).

4

Start the import. Large files process in the background — you'll see the count update as rows land.

💡 Duplicates

Imports merge on matching phone numbers — a re-import updates existing contacts instead of duplicating them.

Tags

Tags are free-form labels for filtering and automations: vip, wholesale, karachi, opted-out…

  • Add or remove tags from a contact's profile, in bulk from the list, or automatically via automations.
  • Tags power segments and campaign audiences — design a small set of consistent tags for your team.

Segments

A segment is a saved, auto-updating filter — “all contacts tagged VIP with an email”, “leads from the last 30 days”.

1

Open Contacts → Segments and click New Segment.

2

Define conditions (tag exists, field equals, date ranges) and save with a clear name.

3

Use the segment as a campaign audience or to bulk-manage members. Members update automatically as contacts change.

Bulk actions

  • Select multiple contacts (or filter first, then select all matches) to tag, untag or delete in one go.
  • Bulk changes are ideal right after an import — tag the whole batch, then let segments and automations take over.

Lead finder

Leads helps you discover new business contacts by location and business type, and save them as contacts to reach out to.

1

Open Leads from the sidebar.

2

Search by area and category (e.g. “DHA Lahore — dentists”).

3

Review results with names, addresses and phone numbers, then save the ones you want as contacts.

⚠️ Message responsibly

Only message people who expect to hear from you. Unsolicited messages cause blocks — and blocks hurt your sender quality on every channel.
