Social Media

Plan, create and schedule posts across your social networks from one composer — with AI help for ideas and captions.

Connecting accounts

Under Social Media → Accounts, connect the pages and profiles you post to — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), YouTube and TikTok, depending on your plan. Each network uses its official login; connected accounts show their status right on the card.

The composer

1

Open Social Media → Composer and pick the accounts to post to (one post can go to several networks at once).

2

Write the caption and attach media. A live preview shows exactly how the post will look on each network.

3

Publish now, or schedule it for the best time. Scheduled posts appear on the calendar and the posts list.

💡 Per-network tweaks

The preview updates per selected network — trim captions to each platform's style before publishing instead of cross-posting blindly.

AI content planner

Stuck for content? Open the AI planner from the posts page:

  • Describe your business and pick the networks and tone.
  • The planner drafts a batch of post ideas with captions you can accept into the composer.
  • Edit anything before scheduling — AI drafts are starting points, not final copy.

Content calendar

The calendar view shows all scheduled and published posts for the month, colour-coded by network. Drag or reschedule from the posts list, and filter by account to review one brand at a time.

📷 Screenshot placeholder: social content calendar month view

Managing posts

Social Media → Posts lists everything with status — draft, scheduled, publishing, published or failed. Open a post to edit drafts and scheduled items, or check the error message on failures (expired connections are the most common cause — reconnect the account and try again).
