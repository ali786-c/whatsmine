Unified Inbox

One shared inbox for WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram — so your team never misses a conversation, no matter where it started.

Inbox layout

The inbox has three panels:

  • Left — conversation list. Every conversation with unread badge, last message preview and channel icon. Use the search box and status filters (Open / Pending / Resolved) to find things fast.
  • Middle — conversation thread. The full message history, including media, voice notes, quoted replies and template messages.
  • Right — contact details. Customer profile, tags, and contact fields. Click the name to open the full contact record.

Replying to messages

1

Select a conversation from the list.

2

Type your reply in the composer at the bottom. Press Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line.

3

Use the toolbar above the composer to attach images, videos, documents or record a voice note with the mic button.

You can also hover any earlier message and use the reply action — your answer appears as a quoted reply, just like in WhatsApp.

💡 Canned replies

Save your team's common answers under Inbox → Canned Replies. While typing, insert one and edit before sending — perfect for greetings, pricing answers and support hours.

Voice notes

  • Click the mic button in the composer to start recording; the timer shows elapsed seconds.
  • Stop to preview the recording, then send — WhatsApp voice notes play natively on the customer's phone.
  • Your browser will ask for microphone permission the first time — click Allow.
📎 Instagram audio

Instagram voice notes are delivered as standard audio bubbles (Meta doesn't support WhatsApp's voice-note bubble), but recording and sending works the same way.

Labels

Labels are coloured tags for conversations — for example Urgent, Awaiting Payment or VIP.

1

Create your team's labels under Inbox → Labels (choose name and colour).

2

In a conversation, open the label picker and attach any labels.

3

Filter the conversation list by label to work through a queue — for example everything tagged Awaiting Payment.

🤖 Automatic label

When the AI chatbot hands a chat over to a human, an amber “Waiting for you” label is attached automatically and removed once an agent replies.

Team assignment

  • Assign a conversation to a teammate from the conversation header — they'll see it in their own queue.
  • Change the status: Open (needs action), Pending (waiting on customer) or Resolved (done).
  • Everyone sees the same inbox in real time — new messages appear without refreshing.

When the AI hands over

If your chatbot is answering and a customer asks for a human:

  • The bot immediately tells the customer an agent is joining (you can customise this message per chatbot).
  • The conversation gets the “Waiting for you” label and stays on top of your queue.
  • The bot stays silent after handover — the human agent owns the thread.

The 24-hour window (WhatsApp & Instagram)

Meta rules: free-form replies are only allowed within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside that window:

  • WhatsApp: send an approved template message to re-open the conversation.
  • Instagram: after the window closes, messages can still be sent with the human-agent tag for up to 7 days.
⚠️ Plan ahead

Set up an automation or a follow-up campaign instead of replying manually outside the window.
