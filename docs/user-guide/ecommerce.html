Ecommerce

Connect your online store, sync products and orders, and message customers at every step — confirmation, shipping and abandoned carts.

Connecting a store

1

Open Ecommerce → Stores and click Add Store.

2

Choose WooCommerce or Shopify and follow the connection steps (site URL & API credentials, or the Shopify connect flow).

3

Once connected, products and orders sync automatically — and customers found by phone or email link to their store records.

📎 What syncs

Products (for sharing in chats), orders with line items and totals, and customers. Sync runs continuously in the background.

Products

Under Ecommerce → Products you can browse the synced catalogue and share product cards right inside WhatsApp conversations — perfect for the inbox when a customer asks “do you have this in blue?”.

In automations, the WooCommerce product and Shopify product nodes let a flow send a product card automatically, and the WhatsApp catalog node shares your catalog.

Orders

Ecommerce → Orders lists every order with its status, items and customer. Open an order to see full details and jump to the customer's conversation.

  • Order events power automations: order placed, fulfilled, cancelled and cart abandoned.
  • Tokens like {{context.order_number}}, {{context.order_total}} and {{context.tracking_url}} personalise those messages.

Store automations

From the Stores page, open WhatsApp Automations for one-click starter flows. Popular set-ups:

FlowTriggerTypical message
Order confirmationOrder placed“Order {{context.order_number}} confirmed — total {{context.order_total}}. Thank you! 🙏”
Shipping updateOrder fulfilled“Your order is on its way 🚚 Track: {{context.tracking_url}}”
Win-backOrder cancelled“Anything go wrong? Here's 15% off your next order: COMEBACK15”
Cart recoveryCart abandoned“Your cart is waiting 🛒 Checkout: {{context.recovery_url}}”
New customer welcomeCustomer created“Welcome! WELCOME10 gives you 10% off your first order 🎁”
💡 Recovery that works

Send the first cart reminder about an hour after abandonment, and a final discount touch a day later — this sequence reliably outperforms a single blast.
