Instagram

Turn Instagram comments into DM conversations, guide followers through multi-step flows and reply with rich carousels.

Connecting Instagram

1

Go to Inbox → Channel Setup and click Connect Instagram.

2

Log in with your Instagram professional (business or creator) account and approve the requested permissions.

3

DMs and comment events arrive in your inbox automatically — the webhook registers itself on first connect, no manual Meta setup needed.

⚠️ Professional account required

Personal Instagram accounts can't connect. Switch to a Business or Creator account in the Instagram app first (it's free).

Comment automations

A comment automation watches your posts and replies to commenters — typically sending them a DM. The classic funnel: comment a keyword → get a DM → unlock a link or content.

1

Open Instagram → Automations and click New Automation.

2

Choose the post or reel to monitor and the keyword that triggers it (e.g. “comment LINK”).

3

Pick what happens next: send a DM message or run a DM flow. Optionally ask the commenter to follow you before unlocking content.

4

Activate it. Every matching comment gets a reply and/or DM automatically.

💡 Funnel logs

Open Instagram → Funnel Logs to see every comment, whether it matched, and what was sent — perfect for checking that your funnel works.

DM flows (multi-step)

DM flows are visual, multi-step conversations: welcome message, buttons, questions and answers — like a mini chatbot inside Instagram DMs.

  • Create flows under Instagram → DM Flows with the visual builder.
  • Add steps: text messages, button choices, and questions that save the answer (name, email, preference…).
  • Attach a flow from any comment automation, or start one from the inbox.
  • The follow ask on step 2 works with the uncapped-gate loop: followers skip ahead, non-followers get a friendly follow prompt first.

Templates (carousels & buttons)

Rich Instagram DMs are built from templates in the Instagram → Templates gallery:

  • Button templates — a message with up to three tappable buttons (open URL, next step).
  • Carousel templates — swipeable product cards with image, title and buttons each.
  • Compose with the live customer-view preview, then send from the inbox or attach to automations and flows.

Good to know

  • DMs are possible only with people who message you first or comment — Instagram doesn't allow cold DMs.
  • Media (photos, audio, video) can be sent in DMs; files are delivered through a public URL that Meta fetches.
  • Outside the 24-hour window, replies use the human-agent tag for up to 7 days after the customer's last message.
