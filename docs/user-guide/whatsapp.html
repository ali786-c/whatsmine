WhatsApp

Connect your WhatsApp Business number, manage message templates, automate replies and add WhatsApp chat to your website.

Connecting WhatsApp

Go to Inbox → Channel Setup. Two connection types are available — pick based on how your number is set up:

Cloud API (official)QR session
Best forBusinesses wanting the official Meta APIQuick start with your existing WhatsApp number
SetupMeta business verification, one-click embedded signupScan a QR code from WhatsApp → Linked Devices
CampaignsFull template broadcast supportDepends on your session type
StabilityHighest — backed by MetaRuns alongside your phone app
1

Click Connect WhatsApp (Cloud API) or Connect WhatsApp QR.

2

For QR: open WhatsApp on your phone → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device → scan the code shown. Keep your phone online until the session syncs.

3

For Cloud API: follow the Meta embedded-signup popup — log in to Facebook, pick your business and number, and approve permissions. The webhook is registered automatically.

4

The channel appears in your inbox — new customer messages start arriving automatically.

⚠️ QR sessions

A QR session runs through your own number and can disconnect if the phone is offline for long or the device is removed. Re-scan from the channel page if the status shows disconnected.

Message templates

Templates are pre-approved message formats you can send outside the 24-hour reply window — for order updates, offers and reminders.

1

Open Messaging → Templates and click New Template.

2

Choose a category (Marketing, Utility or Authentication) and write the body. Add variables like {{1}} for personalisation — e.g. “Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} has shipped!”.

3

Submit for approval. Meta usually reviews within minutes to a few hours. Status shows under Templates.

4

Once approved, use the template in the inbox, in campaigns and in automations.

💡 Approval tips

Be specific and honest with the category, avoid aggressive ALL-CAPS promotions, and use a clear footer with an opt-out line (e.g. “Reply STOP to unsubscribe”).

Auto-replies

Auto-replies answer incoming messages instantly based on keywords — great for FAQs and after-hours responses.

1

Open Messaging → Auto-Replies and click New.

2

Enter trigger keywords (e.g. “price, rates, cost”) and the reply text. Matching is not case-sensitive.

3

Toggle the auto-reply active. It fires on every matching inbound message on the connected number.

📎 Good to know

When a customer explicitly asks for a human, the chatbot stops and hands over — auto-replies and bots never trap a customer in a loop.

Website chat widget

Turn website visitors into WhatsApp conversations with an embeddable chat bubble.

1

Open Messaging → Chat Widget and customise the bubble: colour, greeting text, position and your brand avatar.

2

Copy the generated embed snippet.

3

Paste it just before </body> on your website (or send it to your developer). The bubble appears on your site immediately.

Quality & limits

  • Messaging limits (how many unique customers you can message per day) grow automatically as Meta observes quality traffic.
  • Keep opt-outs honoured, avoid spammy blasts, and monitor template quality — a low-quality rating slows your sending.
  • Check Reports → Campaigns to watch delivery and read rates per broadcast.
