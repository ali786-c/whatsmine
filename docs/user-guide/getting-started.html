Getting Started

Create your account, connect your first channel and send your first message — in about 15 minutes.

1. Create your account

1

Open the sign-up page and enter your name, email and a strong password.

2

Check your inbox for the verification email and click the verification link. Didn't get it? Check spam, then use Resend verification on the login screen.

3

Log in with your new credentials. If two-factor authentication is enabled for your account, enter the code from your authenticator app.

💡 Tip

You can also sign in with a magic link — request it from the login page and click the link we email you, no password needed.

2. Complete the onboarding wizard

After your first login, the onboarding wizard guides you through the essentials. It tracks seven steps:

StepWhat to do
Verify emailConfirm your email address (automatic once you click the link).
Choose a planPick a subscription that matches your team size and volume.
Connect first channelLink WhatsApp (Cloud API or QR), Messenger or Instagram.
Import first contactsAdd contacts manually or import from a CSV/Excel file.
Send first messageUse a campaign or the inbox to start a conversation.
Train first chatbotCreate an AI chatbot and attach a knowledge base.
Connect social accountLink Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X for posting.

Each step links directly to the right page, and you can mark steps complete yourself if you've already done them elsewhere. Once all steps are done the wizard disappears from your dashboard.

3. Dashboard tour

The dashboard is your mission control. What you see here:

  • Stat cards — conversations, contacts, campaign activity and AI usage at a glance.
  • Charts — message volume and social posting trends over time.
  • Quick links — jump straight to the Inbox, Campaigns or Automations.
  • Onboarding widget — visible until you complete all setup steps.
📷 Screenshot placeholder: client dashboard overview

4. Workspaces

A workspace is a separate container for one business, brand or client. Contacts, conversations, campaigns, chatbots and settings are all scoped to a workspace — nothing is shared between them.

  • Go to Account → Workspaces to create additional workspaces (if your plan allows).
  • Switch between workspaces from the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.
  • Each workspace has its own channels, so you can run multiple brands from one login.

5. Profile & preferences

1

Click your avatar (bottom of the sidebar) and open Settings.

2

Update your name, email and password, and upload a profile photo.

3

Choose your language and currency — the entire interface translates instantly.

4

Pick light or dark theme under Theme. The app follows your choice on every device you log in from.
