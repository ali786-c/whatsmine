Reports

Five report views answer the big questions: how fast you reply, what campaigns achieve, what automations do, what AI costs you and how social performs.

Every report has a date-range picker at the top — all charts and totals update to the selected period.

Inbox reports

Reports → Inbox shows conversation health:

  • Volume — inbound and outbound messages over time, per channel.
  • Response times — how quickly your team replies (first reply and overall).
  • Workload — conversations per agent and per status, so you can balance assignment.
💡 Use it weekly

Watch first-response time as your north-star support metric — customers forgive slow resolutions more than slow first replies.

Campaign reports

Reports → Campaigns compares your broadcasts: delivery, read and reply rates per campaign, with trends across the period. Click into a campaign for its per-recipient detail (see Campaigns).

Automation reports

Reports → Automations shows how many runs each automation executed and how they completed — successful runs, waits and failures. A sudden drop in runs usually means the automation got paused or its trigger stopped occurring.

AI usage

Reports → AI tracks chatbot activity: how many replies the AI generated over time and overall usage volume. Use it to watch adoption after launching a new chatbot or knowledge base.

Social reports

Reports → Social summarises your posting activity: posts per network, status breakdown and recent posts. Pair it with the content calendar to keep posting consistent.
